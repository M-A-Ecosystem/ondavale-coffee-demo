Our people

Twelve people make Ondavale run, from the roastery floor to the cafe counter. Our cafe manager has been with us for six years and runs the cafe and the roster. Our assistant roaster roasts the four core blends. Our wholesale and training coordinator looks after our partners and runs barista courses in the training room twice a month.

We pay award rates or better, train everyone who wants to learn to roast or to pour, and close the cafe for the team's Christmas lunch every year.

Barista courses

Our two-group training room hosts courses for beginners and for cafe teams: espresso basics, milk texture and latte art, and filter brewing. Wholesale partners' staff train free.

Ondavale Coffee Roasters, Fremantle, Western Australia. Wholesale enquiries: wholesale@ondavale.example
